Privacy & data practices

Privacy at Havenmere.

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This notice covers this informational website and the internal StrataZone Backups utility. It does not describe a public client portal. Additional notices will be provided if new services collect information in different ways.

Website and contact

This website has no contact form, advertising scripts, or analytics scripts added by Havenmere. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond and manage the conversation. Please do not send passwords, financial account credentials, or sensitive financial documents through ordinary email.

The website hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs under its own privacy practices.

StrataZone Backups and Google Drive

StrataZone Backups is an internal utility operated for Havenmere. Its Google authorization is used to upload encrypted backup archives, list backup files, and verify that stored backup copies match the local files. It is not offered as a public Google Drive application.

The current Google Drive authorization requests the full Drive scope. This permission is broader than the configured backup task requires: the utility is configured to work with a designated backup folder. It does not use Drive documents for advertising or AI model training.

Backup archives can contain application records and operational data. They are encrypted on the server before upload. The backup decryption key is kept separately from the cloud backup copies. Google authorization credentials are stored in a restricted server configuration and are not included on this website.

Sharing and storage providers

Havenmere does not sell Google user data. Google Drive stores the authorized encrypted backup copies; an independent encrypted backup copy is also maintained in Microsoft OneDrive. Email communications are handled through our email provider. These providers process information under their respective terms and privacy policies.

Retention and control

Backup copies are retained for recovery until the operator removes them. There is currently no automatic cloud deletion schedule. Revoking authorization stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete existing backups. The Google account owner can remove the app's access through Google Account connections and can delete stored files directly in Drive.

Google API user data

Havenmere's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Questions and requests

Contact abfin66@outlook.com for questions about these practices, access, or deletion. We may need to verify a requester's authority before acting. This notice may be updated as our services develop.
